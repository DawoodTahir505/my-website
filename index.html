About

Who I am

I'm an aspiring data scientist based in Karachi, Pakistan, focused on turning raw, messy data into stories that hold up under scrutiny — cleaning it, modeling it, and visualizing what it actually means.

I bridge analysis and application: engineering features for a predictive model one day, serving those same insights through a FastAPI endpoint the next. I care about building things that get used, not just notebooks that get read once.

Right now I'm deepening my SQL and scikit-learn fundamentals, with cloud platforms (AWS) next on the list — the goal is a toolkit that travels well across teams and industries.
