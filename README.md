# Dawood — Data Science Student & Aspiring AI Engineer

A minimal, professional personal website for **Dawood**, Data Science student and aspiring AI Engineer.

Built with clean typography, readable prose, and an honest, mature aesthetic.

---

## 🧭 Page Structure

1. **Header**: Minimal navigation with `DAWOOD` title and clean links to `About`, `Skills`, `Interests`, `Certificates`, and `Contact`.
2. **Introduction**: Straightforward introduction with your role, background statement, direct buttons, and links to your LinkedIn, GitHub, and email.
3. **About Me**: Clear explanation of your technical focus in Data Science, Machine Learning, Python, and modern AI development.
4. **Technical Skills**: Simple, readable list of skills grouped cleanly by domain (Programming, Data Analysis, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, AI Development, Tools).
5. **Learning Interests**: Clean bullet points highlighting core exploration areas.
6. **Certifications**: Two clean certificate cards featuring high-resolution images rendered from your credentials, complete with an interactive lightbox modal, zoom capability, and original image links:
   - **Certificate 01**: *Artificial Intelligence and Data Science* (Saylani IT Training Programme)
   - **Certificate 02**: *Python Essentials 2* (Cisco Networking Academy & OpenEDG Python Institute)
7. **Contact**: Direct email with copy button, GitHub, and your LinkedIn profile (`www.linkedin.com/in/dawood-ai`).
8. **Footer**: Minimal copyright and role notice.
